How the model works

Pricing

d₁ = [ln(S/K) + (r − q + σ²/2)T] / (σ√T) d₂ = d₁ − σ√T Call = S·e⁻ᑫᵀ·N(d₁) − K·e⁻ʳᵀ·N(d₂) Put = K·e⁻ʳᵀ·N(−d₂) − S·e⁻ᑫᵀ·N(−d₁)

Units used here

  • Vega and vanna: per 1 volatility point (28% → 29%).
  • Theta and charm: per calendar day (365-day year).
  • Rho: per 1 rate point (4.00% → 5.00%).
  • Implied vol: Newton-Raphson on vega, with a bisection fallback when vega is too small.

Where it breaks down

  • Assumes one constant volatility. Real markets have a skew, so IV differs by strike.
  • European exercise only. American puts and calls on dividend payers can be worth more.
  • Lognormal returns understate crash and gap risk.
  • Your edge is only as good as your volatility forecast.
